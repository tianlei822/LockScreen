Source files are unmodified. Threshold resamples and projects the maps onto spheres,
composites Earth's clouds, and applies decorative directional lighting at runtime.
The publisher bases the maps on NASA elevation and imagery data, with adjusted
colors and fictional terrain in unmapped areas. These are visualization assets,
not a claim of exact present-day appearance or scientifically scaled orbits.
